================================================================================

1. INTRODUCTION & INDUSTRY CONTEXT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Topic Overview:
End-to-end CRM and accounting architecture requires connecting client companies, contacts, sales pipeline deals, proposals, line-item pricing, inventory costs, supplier purchase orders, and ledger financial transactions within a multi-tenant isolation model.

Needs & Business Pains:
SaaS and agency platforms often suffer from disconnected operational silos: sales reps track deals in a lightweight CRM, proposals are sent as un-tracked PDFs, and accounting calculates invoices in separate software. This leads to untracked receivables, inaccurate profit margins, and broken audit trails.

Challenges:
- Complete Lifecycle Traceability: Tracking a lead from initial brief intake -> CRM deal -> client proposal -> approved deal -> supplier purchase order -> invoice issuance.
- Multi-Tenant Financial Auditability: Ensuring financial transactions, tax computations, and billing records strictly enforce tenant boundaries without cross-tenant exposure.
- State Machine Integrity: Preventing illegal financial state jumps (e.g. issuing an invoice for an unapproved deal or marking a proposal paid before creation).

Common Gaps & Mistakes:
- Storing currency amounts as floating-point numbers instead of integer cents or fixed-decimal numeric types.
- Lacking atomic state transitions on deal stages and financial statuses.
- Storing invoices as un-audited mutable records instead of immutable ledger transactions.

Common Solutions Available & What We Can Do Better:
- Standard Solution: Basic CRM pipeline with third-party webhooks to accounting software.
- What We Can Do Better: State-Machine Driven CRM & Accounting Engine: state_transitions table enforcing valid status flows, deal pipeline stages bound to proposals, immutable proposal_items line item snapshots, and automated margin calculations.


2. PLATFORM CONTEXT & OPTIMAL USAGE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
What Exists in Our Platform:
- Pipeline Schemas (backend/src/backend/migrations.sql): deals, contacts, crm_customers, status_library, state_transitions, proposals, proposal_items, pdf_queue.
- Financial Routing (backend/src/backend/main.py): /api/v1/proposals/generate, /api/v1/proposals/public/{token}, /api/v1/deals/{deal_id}/stage.
- State Engine (backend/src/backend/migrations.sql Section 2 & 10): enforce_state_transition() SQL trigger on document state changes.

Best Way to Use It:
- Represent sales opportunities in deals (linked to crm_customers and contacts).
- Generate structured commercial proposals using /api/v1/proposals/generate, which creates proposal_items line item cost snapshots.
- Enforce valid lifecycle transitions (draft -> pending_approval -> approved -> invoiced) via state_transitions and enforce_state_transition() database triggers.


3. BACKEND SENSITIVE POINTS NEEDING ATTENTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Integer / Fixed-Precision Currency Math: All price calculations (cost_price, margin, tax, total) must compute using exact decimal precision (or integer cents) to prevent rounding drift.
- Atomic State Transition Guards: Prevent deals or invoices from moving to "completed" or "invoiced" unless all required line items have passed stock verification (/api/v1/inventory/verify-stock).
- Financial RLS Isolation: Ensure deals, proposals, and ledger records enforce tenant_id = current_tenant_id() across 100% of read/write queries.


4. FRONTEND SENSITIVE POINTS NEEDING ATTENTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Single-Row Deal Summary: Render deal title, client company, pipeline stage, total value, gross margin %, and action button on a single compact row.
- Real-Time Margin Indicator: Display live gross margin percentage chips (Green = >30%, Yellow = 15-30%, Red = <15%) as sales reps build proposals.
- Lifecycle Stage Progress Bar: Render an interactive pipeline progress stepper (Intake -> Proposal -> Review -> Approved -> Invoiced).


5. WHAT MUST BE WIRED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Wire deals to crm_customers (customer_id FK) and contacts (primary_contact_id FK).
- Wire proposals to deals (deal_id FK) and proposal_items line items.
- Wire invoice generation events to pdf_queue for asynchronous background PDF rendering.


6. WHAT MUST BE HARDWIRED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Hardwire Postgres RLS on deals, proposals, and proposal_items.
- Hardwire database state transition triggers (enforce_state_transition()) to block illegal status skips.
- Hardwire immutable transaction logging for all financial status updates.
