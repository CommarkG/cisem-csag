Purpose
Defines the bounded marketing-platform reference project used to validate CISEM through real implementation. The initial executable scope is the product-image processing pipeline; later cycles may add knowledge extraction, product grouping, search, campaign assets and personalized offers only through approved CoreCycles.


Canonical Scope
Owns the project goal, project-level navigation, active CoreCycle order, platform-selection record and project completion state. Universal governance remains in the CISEM Core files.

Project Goal
Transform approximately 3,000 heterogeneous corporate-award product images into a governed, searchable and reusable marketing asset system. The system must preserve originals, identify same-product image sets, normalize presentation, extract structured product knowledge, and later support websites, offers, campaigns and content generation.


Selected Implementation Platform
Google Antigravity is selected for the first implementation trial because the current need combines repository work, browser-visible UI, pipeline orchestration and iterative artifact review. Selection is provisional and must be re-evaluated when evidence changes.

Active Finish Line
A single raw product image can be submitted through a visible frontend, processed through a traceable backend pipeline, returned as a 1080×1080 normalized image on a bright surface with controlled reflection and shadow, reviewed against explicit criteria, and preserved with metadata and evidence without altering the original.


CoreCycle Advancement Rule
No later image grouping, tagging, batch scaling or supplier harvesting begins until CoreCycle 01 satisfies its acceptance criteria and survives controlled variation.
