## S1 · Local — what was found

**Where:** the pipeline's front end. An actor received an intent and began designing.

**The change:** `P1.2 Align` inserted as its own stage. The actor restates the intent, lists what it **excluded**, lists what it **assumed**, and waits for explicit human confirmation. Correction restarts the stage in full.

**Local scope, if we had stopped here:** one method gained one stage. Everything else in the system continued to assume understanding.

## S3a · Root — the extraction

**Why did this arise?** Because understanding was assumed at a boundary.

**The general truth:**

> Wherever understanding transfers between two parties, the receiver must state what it understood — including what it **excluded** — and the sender must confirm explicitly.

**Why it qualifies as a jewel** (`U1.2.37.3`): it is true of a doctor and a patient, a contract and a builder, a caller and an authentication system. It has nothing to do with pipelines.

**Why it is high-leverage:** unsurfaced divergence is the **only** condition where everything downstream works perfectly and serves a different goal. Nothing downstream catches it, because there is nothing to catch — the work is internally flawless.

**Named once at `U1.2.38`.**
