===============================================================================
1 · THE THREE TERMS
===============================================================================

A CORESPINE IS THE ORDERED CHAIN OF SETTLED ELEMENTS.
Each element, once settled, binds everything that comes after it. It is not a
list of topics. It is a sequence in which position carries obligation.

A CORE CYCLE IS ONE PASS ALONG THAT CHAIN.
Not a task, not a sprint, not a plan version. ONE PASS, over one element, at one
depth.

A SPIRAL IS RETURNING TO AN ELEMENT ALREADY SETTLED, LATER, AT GREATER DEPTH.
Revisiting identity once locations exist and finding what that changes.
NINETEEN VERSIONS OF ONE PLAN IS ONE CYCLE REFINED NINETEEN TIMES. IT IS NOT A
SPIRAL. The distinction matters because refinement improves an answer while a
spiral changes what the question was.

AX-SPIRAL-01 · NON-RIGID CORECYCLES.
  A cycle carries no fixed functional definition. There is no rule that cycle
  three is the interface cycle or cycle one the database cycle. CYCLES ARE
  DEFINED BY CONTEXT AND BY INHERITANCE — deep foundations first, each cycle
  building outwards from what is already settled.

AX-SPIRAL-02 · FLEXIBLE PILLAR LIFECYCLES.
  An architectural concern need not appear in every cycle. It may begin or end
  in any cycle according to the state of the work. A CRITICAL PILLAR MAY NOT
  APPEAR UNTIL THE THIRD OR FOURTH CYCLE because it depends on capabilities that
  must exist and be verified first.

AX-SPIRAL-03 · VARIABLE MATURITY EXIT.
  A subject may reach maturity at any point and simply not appear again. Once
  verified as mature it becomes a read-only stable dependency.
  NOTE ADDED IN V3: maturity here means one element hardening. IT IS NOT DEPTH.
  See section 5.

begin, mature or end in it · and its EXECUTABLE PROOF, the mechanical validation

===============================================================================
2 · THE FOUR PROPERTIES OF A CORE CYCLE
===============================================================================
A CYCLE CARRIES ALL FOUR AT ONCE. Three are disciplines. One can be enforced.

ORDER · IT HAS A PLACE IN THE CHAIN AND CANNOT JUMP IT.
  A cycle addressing what a thing CARRIES before what a thing IS produces
  specification that must be unwound. On 2026-08-22 a plan proposed contact
  attributes before it was settled what a contact attaches to; where a person
  attaches decides whether an address belongs to them or to their organisation,
  and storing it on the wrong one means the same address stored ten times and
  drifting.

DEPTH · IT GOES AS DEEP AS THE QUESTION REQUIRES AND NO DEEPER.
  A cycle that answers a question nobody asked has spent specification on a
  layer beneath it that is not yet settled. THAT SPECIFICATION IS UNWOUND LATER,
  ALWAYS AT GREATER COST THAN IT SAVED.

PROGRESSIVE SPECIFICATION · FROM WHAT A THING IS, TO WHAT IT CARRIES, TO HOW IT
  BEHAVES. NEVER THE REVERSE.
  Identity before attributes. Attributes before rules. Rules before interfaces.

A STRICT NON-OPTIONAL OBLIGATION TO EVERY PREVIOUS ELEMENT ON ITS CORESPINE.
  THIS IS THE ONLY PROPERTY THAT CAN BE MECHANICALLY ENFORCED, AND NOTHING
  ENFORCES IT TODAY.
  A cycle that contradicts a settled element is refused, not debated. On
  2026-08-24 two tables were prepared whose isolation policy read a static token
  claim — CONTRADICTING AN ELEMENT SETTLED TWO DAYS EARLIER AND PROVEN BY FIVE
  ATTEMPTS. Nothing stopped it. It was caught by a person reading a report.


===============================================================================
3 · THE CORESPINE AS IT STANDS TODAY
===============================================================================
IN ORDER. EACH BINDS EVERYTHING AFTER IT.

  1 · WHO. One identity, many operational records. An operational record may not
      exist without an identity behind it — ENFORCED IN SCHEMA 2026-08-22. A
      contact attaches to a counterparty and the attachment is optional; a lead,
      a freelancer and a person whose company is unknown are all real.
  2 · ISOLATION. A tenant cannot read another tenant, write into another tenant,
      rewrite platform reference data, publish a row visible to everyone, or
      delete another tenant. PROVEN BY ATTEMPT IN FIVE DIRECTIONS 2026-08-22,
      every one refused by the database.
  3 · THE IDENTITY OF THE CALLER. No request runs as an administrator. The role
      comes from the caller's own verified token. LANDED 2026-08-22.

ANY CYCLE THAT CONTRADICTS ONE OF THOSE THREE IS REFUSED.

AN ELEMENT IS SETTLED WHEN IT HAS LANDED AND BEEN OBSERVED — re-read on a fresh
connection, or seen acting by a party that did not build it. RATIFIED IS NOT
SETTLED. AGREED IS NOT SETTLED.

AND SETTLED MUST BE RECORDED, NOT REMEMBERED. ADDED IN V2 ON ANTIGRAVITY'S
FINDING. Today the three elements above are settled because two agents and the
Governor recall them being proven. THAT IS A CLAIM CARRIED IN CONVERSATION, AND
CONVERSATION IS THE TIER THAT FAILS — a whole mechanism was lost between chats
on 2026-08-21 for exactly that reason, and a core cycle governed a full day of
work while existing only as a header string.
A COMMITTED RECORD NAMES EACH SETTLED ELEMENT, WHAT PROVED IT, AND WHEN. Until
it exists, every obligation in this document rests on memory.

AN ELEMENT CANNOT BE VERIFIED AGAINST ONE INSTANCE. ADDED IN V2, AND IT IS THE
FINDING THAT REORDERED A PLAN.
Isolation cannot be observed with one tenant — a refusal needs something to be
refused FROM. The same holds generally: a rule about relationships cannot be
proven with one party, and a rule about states cannot be proven in one state.
WHAT THIS CHANGES IN PRACTICE: the thing that looks like the last step is often
the test rig for the first. Three real companies were placed fifth in a plan and
belong second, because the rewrite placed first cannot be proven without them.


===============================================================================
4 · HOW A CYCLE IS ORDERED AGAINST OTHERS
===============================================================================

A THING COMES FIRST IF WHAT FOLLOWS INHERITS FROM IT.

THREE THINGS MAKE SOMETHING FIRST:
  · EVERYTHING DOWNSTREAM IS BUILT ON ITS MECHANISM. Build on the wrong one and
    the work is not wrong — it is built on sand and must be redone.
  · IT IS FREE NOW AND EXPENSIVE LATER. Every constraint on an empty table is
    one statement. On a populated table it is a migration, a backfill and a
    ruling about every row with no value. CHEAPNESS IS A WINDOW, NOT A PROPERTY.
  · IT PREVENTS THE NEXT INSTANCE OF SOMETHING THAT HAS ALREADY HAPPENED
    REPEATEDLY.

WHAT DOES NOT MAKE SOMETHING FIRST: that it is visible, that it was promised, or
that it would feel like progress.


===============================================================================
5 · WHAT IS NOT ENFORCED, STATED SO IT IS NOT MISTAKEN FOR MECHANISM
===============================================================================

  · NOTHING REFUSES A CYCLE THAT CONTRADICTS A SETTLED ELEMENT. The fourth
    property is a discipline today and it has already failed once.
  · NOTHING DISTINGUISHES A SECOND PASS FROM A FIRST. A spiral cannot be seen in
    the record, so depth is invisible and nobody can tell refinement from
    return.
    AND DEPTH IS NOT MATURITY. RULED BY THE GOVERNOR 2026-08-24, AFTER A
    PROPOSAL CONFLATED THEM.
    MATURITY is the same answer becoming more solid — a structure exists, then
    it is isolated, then it is tested. It is one element hardening.
    DEPTH is returning to a settled element and FINDING SOMETHING THAT WAS NOT
    VISIBLE THE FIRST TIME — revisiting identity once locations exist and
    discovering that a person's home address cannot live where a company's
    office lives.
    CONFLATING THEM MEANS A SPIRAL CAN NEVER BE DISTINGUISHED FROM ORDINARY
    HARDENING, and the methodology loses the only thing that makes it a spiral.
    BOTH ARE WORTH RECORDING. NEITHER IS THE OTHER.
  · NOTHING RECORDS WHAT A CYCLE INHERITS. The obligation is carried in
    somebody's memory, and memory is the tier that fails.
  · A NAMED CORE CYCLE WITH NO DOCUMENT BEHIND IT PASSES EVERY CHECK. That is
    how a day of work was governed by a citation.

THE PROPOSED SHAPE, NOT YET DESIGNED AND NOT AUTHORISED HERE: a cycle declares
the elements it inherits; a check refuses one that contradicts a settled
element; depth is a number that makes a second pass visible. The closure tables
already answer ancestry in one query and may be the shape.


===============================================================================
6 · WHAT DEFEATS THIS
===============================================================================

  · A CHAIN THAT GROWS TOO LONG TO READ. Nobody inherits what nobody reads.
  · AN ELEMENT QUIETLY REOPENED. Settled must mean settled, or the obligation
    means nothing.
  · A CYCLE THAT DECLARES AN INHERITANCE IT DOES NOT HONOUR. A declaration is a
    word, and a check satisfied by a word is not a check.
  · INVENTING VOCABULARY BESIDE IT. These three terms are ratified and in use.
    THE WORK IS GIVING THEM MECHANISM, NOT NAMES.
  · TWO DOCUMENTS DESCRIBING ONE METHODOLOGY. IT HAPPENED — a 61-line
    specification sat in the repository from 2026-08-10 while this file was
    written from scratch, and both parties cited "the CoreSpiral" for days
    holding different documents. V3 IS THE MERGE. THERE IS ONE FILE.
  · A MECHANISM LARGER THAN THE DEFINITION IT ENFORCES. A validator requiring a
    declaration header on every plan and code file, checked by a new gate phase,
    is a large structure built on a definition ratified hours earlier. IT MAY BE
    RIGHT. IT IS NOT SMALL, AND IT IS NOT AUTHORISED HERE.

THE RULE THIS DOCUMENT LIVES BY: A FINDING THAT CHANGES A STANDING DOCUMENT
PRODUCES A NEW VERSION OF IT IN THE SAME TURN. Not later, not batched. The
version moves, the change is named in the header, the old one survives as
evidence. IMPROVEMENT THAT LIVES ONLY IN A CONVERSATION IS IMPROVEMENT THAT WILL
BE LOST — fifteen documents were lost that way on 2026-08-22.
